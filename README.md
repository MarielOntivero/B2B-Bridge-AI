# B2B Bridge AI

### From thousands of companies to the few that matter.

B2B Bridge AI is an AI-assisted B2B business discovery and matchmaking platform designed to help companies identify business opportunities they might otherwise miss.

## The Problem

Sales teams have access to thousands of potential companies, but limited time to research them.

Even at major technology and industrial trade fairs, sales teams cannot meet every relevant company. Valuable opportunities can be missed because of limited time, incomplete market knowledge, or difficulty identifying the right prospects.

Traditional CRM systems are mainly designed to manage opportunities once they have been identified.

B2B Bridge AI focuses on the step before that:

*Discovering the opportunities.*

## The Concept

A company provides information about:

•⁠  ⁠What it offers
•⁠  ⁠What it is looking for
•⁠  ⁠Target industries
•⁠  ⁠Target markets
•⁠  ⁠Geographic priorities

B2B Bridge AI analyzes publicly available company information and identifies potential business matches.

For each potential match, the system aims to explain:

•⁠  ⁠Why the companies may be compatible
•⁠  ⁠Product and technology fit
•⁠  ⁠Market and geographic fit
•⁠  ⁠Potential business opportunity
